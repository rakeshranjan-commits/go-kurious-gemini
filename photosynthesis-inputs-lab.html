Lab Missions

Observation

The plant has enough light, water, and carbon dioxide to make glucose and release oxygen.

CO2 + H2O + light → glucose + O2Leaves use chlorophyll to capture light energy.
The process

Inside a green leaf

1

Roots absorb water.
Water travels upward through tubes in the stem.

2

Leaves take in carbon dioxide.
Tiny stomata allow gases to enter and leave.

3

Chlorophyll captures sunlight.
Light energy powers the rearrangement of molecules.

4

Glucose stores energy.
The plant uses this food for growth and releases oxygen.

Evidence

Test a leaf for starch

Cover part of a leaf with opaque paper, keep the plant in sunlight, then perform the iodine test with adult supervision. Only the exposed green area turns blue-black because starch formed there.

Light energy → chemical energyGlucose may be stored as starch.
Key idea

More of one input cannot compensate for a missing essential input. Photosynthesis is limited by the scarcest requirement.

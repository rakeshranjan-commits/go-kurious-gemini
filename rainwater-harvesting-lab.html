Observation

Collection rule

Litres collected = rainfall (mm) x roof area (m2) x efficiency.

Four parts of the system

1

Catchment roof
Receives rainfall over a wide surface.

2

Gutter and downpipe
Carry runoff without spilling it.

3

Leaf filter
Removes twigs, dust, and debris before storage.

4

Storage tank
Holds water for gardens, cleaning, or groundwater recharge.

Concepts

Complete circuitCurrent has an unbroken path from battery to bulb and back.
Open circuitA switch gap or missing wire stops current.
Incomplete wiringAll parts are present, but the path is not connected correctly.

Observation

Tap one terminal, then another terminal to connect a wire. Close the switch after all wires are in place.

Tip: complete path is battery + to switch, switch to bulb, bulb back to battery -.

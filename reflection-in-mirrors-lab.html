Image Properties

Plane mirrors always keep these rules.

Image forms behind the mirror.

Image is virtual, upright, and same size.
Left and right appear interchanged.
Need 2 m closer
Mirror Rule Object distance = image distance
Rays Reflected rays seem to come from the image.
Notice Moving the object moves the image by the same amount.

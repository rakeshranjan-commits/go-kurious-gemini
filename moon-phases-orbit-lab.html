Observation

The Moon does not make light. Its bright part reflects sunlight.

Myth buster

Earth's shadow does not cause normal moon phases. An eclipse is a separate alignment event.

Why the shape changes

Half of the Moon is always illuminated by the Sun. As the Moon orbits, we see changing portions of that bright half.

Build a home model

1

Darken a room and place a lamp at one side to represent the Sun.

2

Hold a white ball at arm's length. Your head represents Earth.

3

Turn slowly while keeping the ball in front of you. Observe its changing bright shape.

4

Record the sequence. A full cycle takes about 29.5 days in space.
